# Neural Differential Equation Models for Time Series

This repository contains a sequence of notebooks developed for a Neural ODE software project course. The notebooks build up a coherent story: starting from deterministic neural ODEs in data space, moving to stochastic extensions, and finally to latent-space models designed to handle irregular sampling, missing data, and distribution shift.

The overall theme is **how different continuous-time formulations trade off expressivity, uncertainty modeling, and robustness**.

---

## Repository Structure and Conceptual Flow

### 1. Neural ODEs for Data-Space Modeling
**Notebook:** `Neural_Ordinary_Differential_Equations_for_Data_Space_Modeling.ipynb`

This notebook introduces neural ODEs as deterministic continuous-time models learned directly in observation space. A neural network parameterizes the vector field  
\[
\frac{dy}{dt} = f_\theta(t, y),
\]
and trajectories are generated by numerical integration.

Experiments progress from:
- A synthetic sine wave (sanity check),
- The lynx–hare population dynamics,
- Real-world COVID-19 case data.

This notebook establishes the baseline behavior and limitations of deterministic ODEs, especially their strong smoothness bias and lack of uncertainty modeling.

---

### 2. Neural SDEs for Data-Space Modeling
**Notebook:** `Neural_Stochastic_Differential_Equations_for_Data_Space_Modeling.ipynb`

This notebook extends the data-space ODE formulation to **Neural SDEs**, introducing stochasticity via a diffusion term:
\[
dx_t = f_\theta(t, x_t)\,dt + g_\phi(t, x_t)\,dW_t.
\]

Key ideas:
- The model defines a *distribution over trajectories* rather than a single path.
- Training uses a likelihood-based objective instead of MSE.
- Evaluation is performed on a known Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process, allowing direct comparison between learned and analytical mean/variance.

This notebook highlights when stochastic dynamics are essential for capturing uncertainty and noisy systems.

---

### 3. Latent Neural ODEs for Irregular Time Series
**Notebook:** `Latent_Neural_ODEs_for_Irregular_Time_Series.ipynb`

Here the focus shifts from data-space modeling to **latent dynamics**. Observations are encoded into a latent state that evolves continuously between measurements.

The core architecture is a **GRU-ODE**:
- A GRU updates the latent state at observation times.
- A neural ODE evolves the latent state between observations.
- A decoder maps the final latent state to the prediction target.

The task is regression on a synthetic spiral dataset with varying levels of missingness. This notebook demonstrates how latent ODEs naturally handle irregular sampling without explicit imputation.

---

### 4. Robustness of Latent Stochastic Dynamics Under Missingness Shift
**Notebook:** `Robustness_of_Latent_Stochastic_Dynamics_Under_Missingness_Shift.ipynb`

This notebook studies robustness under distribution shift caused by changes in observation density.

Two latent models are compared:
- A deterministic Neural CDE (baseline),
- A stochastic Neural LSDE with additive latent noise.

Both models are trained on fully observed data and evaluated on progressively sparser datasets. Performance is analyzed as a function of missingness.

The key result is that **latent stochastic dynamics degrade more slowly under missingness**, supporting the idea that stochasticity acts as a regularizer in latent space.

---

### 5. LSDE on Real-World Clinical Data (Sepsis)
**Notebook:** `LSDE_sepsis.ipynb`

The final notebook applies a latent stochastic differential equation model to the PhysioNet Sepsis dataset, a highly irregular and partially observed clinical time series classification task.

Highlights:
- Explicit separation of observation values and observation intensity.
- Latent stochastic dynamics trained via backpropagation through an SDE solver.
- Comparison against a GRU-ODE baseline under identical preprocessing.

Results show improved robustness and competitive AUROC, demonstrating that the ideas explored in synthetic settings transfer to real-world data.

---

## Takeaway

Across the notebooks, the progression is:

**Neural ODEs → Neural SDEs → Latent ODEs → Latent SDEs**

Each step adds modeling capacity:
- Deterministic → stochastic dynamics,
- Data-space → latent-space representations,
- Regular sampling → irregular and missing observations.

Together, the notebooks provide a practical and empirical exploration of modern continuous-time models for time series.
